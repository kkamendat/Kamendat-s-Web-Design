About Me

Hi, I’m Kayla Kamendat

I’m the creator of Kamendat's Web Design. I recently graduated from the University of Michigan-Flint with a Bachelor’s in Computer Information Systems. During my studies, I discovered my passion for building websites and helping small businesses succeed online.

Current Projects

I’m currently creating websites for Blue Water Pool Services and a custom wedding website, while also managing and updating The Horror Pop Extravaganza: Podcast website. These projects have allowed me to strengthen my skills in web design, development, content management, and creating modern, user-friendly experiences.

My Mission

My goal is to help small businesses bring their ideas to life online. I focus on creating bright, engaging, and functional websites that not only look great but also attract more customers.

Looking Forward

I’m excited to grow my skills and expand my services. I would love to work with more businesses and entrepreneurs to create websites that truly represent their brand.
